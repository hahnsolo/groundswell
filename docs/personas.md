# Who Groundswell is for

Four people we design and test for. Every story on the board names one of them, so we keep asking "would this work for *them*?" They're made up, but each stands for real people we expect to see.

## Maya, the new neighbour
- 34, rents an apartment, uses her phone for everything.
- Noticed one problem (a dark path, a dangerous crossing) and wants to know if anyone else cares.
- Has never organized anything and doesn't think of herself as political.
- **Leaves if:** it's confusing, asks for too much up front, or feels like a petition site that spams her.
- **Test with her in mind:** phone size, first visit, signed out.

## Dev, the organizer
- 45, already runs a tenants' group with email and group chats.
- Wants people to actually show up, and wants the group to look credible to the city.
- Uses a laptop for organizing, his phone on the go.
- **Leaves if:** it's slower than a group chat, or he can't see who's in and who's coming.
- **Test with him in mind:** a busy cause, events, chat, campaigns.

## Rosa, the long-time resident
- 71, owns her home, has lived in the neighbourhood for 30 years. Uses a tablet with large text.
- Careful about privacy and scams; reads everything before agreeing.
- Knows the neighbourhood's history and the local councillor's name.
- **Leaves if:** words are jargon, buttons are small, or it's unclear what happens to her information.
- **Test with her in mind:** large text, dark mode, plain words, privacy pages.

## Jordan, the steward
- Whoever runs Groundswell: owner, admin and moderator.
- Wants it safe, free or cheap to run, and easy to look after without being a full-time developer.
- **Worried about:** spam, abuse, a mistake on the live database, surprise bills.
- **Test with them in mind:** the admin area, the handbook, what happens when something breaks.

## Writing a story

> As **[person]**, I want **[something]**, so that **[why it matters to them]**.

Then add:
- **Acceptance criteria:** what's true when it works, checkable by anyone using the site ("Given…, when…, then…" helps).
- **Test criteria:** which unit tests, click-through checks and by-hand checks prove it.
- **Definition of done:** the standard checklist, included in the feature form.
